# Examples cheat sheet

_Private. Not shared with the interviewee. Use only if the conversation stalls._

## Sample walkthrough prompt

If they struggle with "walk me through a typical job," offer one:

> "Some builders describe it as: foundations and slab, framing, roof on,
> windows and wrap, rough-in (sparky + plumber + data), insulation and gib,
> stopping and painting, flooring, finish trades (skirting, hardware, fit-off),
> final clean and handover. Does that roughly match your world, or is yours
> different?"

## Concept examples

- **Job** — "The Johnson reno at 42 Nikau Street." A specific site. 40-50 active at once.
- **Stage** — "Gib stopping at the Johnson place." One step, one trade, one chunk of time.
- **Trade** — "Sparky." Not Dave the sparky — the category. Sparky, plumber, chippy, roofer, gib stopper, painter, tiler, drainlayer.
- **Crew** — "Grant and his two apprentices" or "the guys from Apex Plumbing." The actual people who show up. Only matters if in-house and you need to distinguish between crews of the same trade.
- **Duration** — "Gib fixing takes 2-3 days on a standard three-bedroom." Fixed per stage type or varies per job size.
- **Deadline** — "They want to be in by Christmas." Common soft target, or hard: "tenants move in 15 November."

## Common NZ/AU trades (prompts only)

Earthworks/drainage, slab/foundations, framing (chippy), roofing, windows/glazing, exterior cladding, plumbing rough-in, electrical rough-in, data/low-voltage, insulation, gib fixing, gib stopping, interior carpentry (skirting/architraves/hardware), waterproofing, tiling, flooring, painting, kitchen install, joinery, shower glass, landscaping, fencing, final clean, inspection/CCC.

## Edge case prompts

If constraints section is too quiet:

> "What's the last job that went off the rails? What happened?"
>
> "If it rains for two weeks straight, what breaks?"
>
> "Has a subcontractor ever gone AWOL mid-job? What did you do?"
>
> "Do any stages have mandatory waiting periods? Gib stopping drying, concrete curing, that sort of thing?"
>
> "Are any trades shared with commercial jobs, or are they all on residential?"
>
> "What happens when the council inspection gets booked out for three weeks?"

## Solver objective prompts

If it's unclear whether the goal is speed or reliability:

> "If you had to choose: every job hits its handover date but some crews sit
> idle some days — or every crew is busy every day but some jobs run late.
> Which would you pick?"
