# Questions

## Opening

1. **What makes this hard right now?** What's the pain?
2. **What does the owner do today?** Whiteboard, spreadsheet, gut feel?
3. **What data exists already?** Spreadsheets, invoices, anything digital?
4. **What question can't they answer today** that they wish they could?
5. **What does "managing" the jobs mean?** Visibility into current state? Actively scheduling and rescheduling? Both?

## Domain model

6. **Walk me through a typical job.** What are the stages?
7. **Do stages always happen in the same order?**
8. **Are stages a fixed template per job type,** or does every job have unique stages?
9. **How many different trades are there?**
10. **In-house crews or subcontractors?**
11. **One crew per trade, or multiple crews?**
12. **Do stages have fixed durations, or do they vary per job?**

## Workflow

13. **Who's the primary user?** Just the owner, or tradespeople and project managers too?
14. **When does someone look at this?** Morning standup, weekly planning, when something breaks?
15. **What's the trigger to check the system?** Proactive (daily routine) or reactive (something went wrong)?

## Constraints and edge cases

16. **Do trades have availability constraints?** Only certain days, part-time, seasonal?
17. **Can a trade work on two sites in one day,** or is it one site per day?
18. **Are buffer times needed between stages?** E.g. concrete curing for 2 days.
19. **What happens when something goes wrong?** Trade delayed, weather, a job added last minute.

## Capstone

20. **What would make the owner say "this is exactly what I needed"?**
